About Whitetail Studios

Where passion for wood meets precision craftsmanship.

The Beginning

Built on a Love of Wood

What started as a hobby quickly became a passion. Whitetail Studios was born from a simple idea: that every piece of wood has a story, and it deserves to be told through expert craftsmanship.

We source premium hardwoods — walnut, oak, maple, and more — from local suppliers, ensuring each log meets our standards for grain character, figure, and structural integrity.

Our Process

Every piece that leaves our shop goes through a meticulous process. From selecting the right log, to precision milling on our Wood-Mizer sawmill, to drying, shaping, and finishing — nothing is rushed.

We specialize in live edge furniture, epoxy resin work, and custom cuts. Whether it's a statement coffee table or a set of floating shelves, we bring the same level of care and attention to every project.
