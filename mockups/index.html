Our Process

From Forest to Furniture

01

Source

We select premium hardwood logs — walnut, oak, maple — sourcing locally for the best grain character.

02

Mill

Each log is milled on our Wood-Mizer sawmill, precision-cut to preserve the natural live edge and figure.

03

Craft

Slabs are dried, shaped, and finished into custom furniture — with epoxy, metal bases, and flawless detail.
